Logo Usage

The Nivanto logo is the most visible expression of the brand. These rules protect its integrity across every surface — digital, print, and social — so it always reads with the same trustworthiness.

1.1 Logo Anatomy

Two components: the Symbol (arc + dot) and the Wordmark. They function as a single unit in the full logo lockup.

Arc Split curve — growth,
support, forward motion
Symbol Arc + dot together,
never separated
NIVANTO
Dot Confidence, vision,
the guiding point
Wordmark Syne 800 · 7px
letter-spacing · all caps
1.2 Logo Versions

Three approved versions. Choose based on the background color. Never combine elements from different versions.

Primary
NIVANTO
Reversed
NIVANTO
Symbol Only

Blue or Amber variant
for compact spaces

✓Use Primary on white (#FFFFFF) and brand background (#F8F6F2)
✓Use Reversed on dark (#1C2440) and primary blue (#2B5FAA)
✓Symbol Only for app icons, favicons, profile photos, embossed/embroidered use
✕Symbol Only is a fallback — use the full lockup wherever space allows
1.3 Clear Space

The minimum exclusion zone on all four sides equals X — the diameter of the dot. No text, icons, or other graphics may enter this zone.

1.4 Minimum Size
